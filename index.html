Chapter I • 汉字之美

The Architecture of Mandarin

声调与部首

Chinese is not just an alphabet—it is a musical, visual language built upon four melodic tones and ancient pictographic radicals. Each tone gives the same syllable entirely different meanings!

Interactive 4 Tones Soundboard (Test with 'Ma') Click each tone to listen!

日 + 月 = 明
Sun + Moon = Bright (Míng)

When the two celestial luminaries stand together, you get radiant brightness.

木 + 木 = 林
Tree + Tree = Woods (Lín)

Two trees form a woods. Add a third (森) and you get a dense forest.

女 + 子 = 好
Woman + Child = Good (Hǎo)

The mother holding her child represents harmony and goodness.
