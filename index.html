The Soul of Chinese

Chinese is an ancient, pictographic language where characters tell visual stories:

日 (Sun) + 月 (Moon) = 明 (Bright)
When the celestial lights combine, you achieve ultimate clarity.
木 (Tree) + 木 (Tree) = 林 (Woods)
Two trees form a woods; three trees (森) become a dense forest.
女 (Woman) + 子 (Child) = 好 (Good)
The mother holding her child signifies universal harmony.
